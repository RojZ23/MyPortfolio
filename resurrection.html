The Game

Resurrection: Second Lives is a story-driven survival horror game set in a quarantined city. It pairs atmospheric, cinematic storytelling with tense survival gameplay, and the narrative and technical systems were designed together so the story and the mechanics support each other.

I lead the project and develop it in Unreal Engine 5. Development began in September 2024, and the project was upgraded from Unreal Engine 5.4 to 5.7 along the way. I also lead a team of voice actors and concept artists.
